# Application overview: ServiceDesk

**Application ID:** `servicedesk`  
**Application:** ServiceDesk  
**Domain:** IT service management  
**Demo owner:** IT Service Management Team  
**Escalation role:** IT Operations Manager  
**Demo access label:** Internal  
**Data status:** Helios Demo Enterprise Data; synthetic

## Purpose

ServiceDesk is the fictional Helios platform for employee support tickets, incidents, assets, internal
knowledge references, and operational notifications.

## Main capabilities

- Submit and track support requests.
- Classify, prioritize, assign, and resolve incidents.
- Track laptops, software, and other employee assets.
- Link tickets to approved troubleshooting knowledge.
- Notify requesters and support teams about status changes.

## Application boundary

ServiceDesk owns IT support workflows and asset records. It may reference employee identity from
PeopleHub, but it does not own HR leave or payroll data.
