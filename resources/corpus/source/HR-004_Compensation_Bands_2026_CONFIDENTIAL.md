# Compensation Bands 2026 (Confidential)

Document ID: HR-004 | Department: HR | Category: REPORT | Owner: Total Rewards | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — HR only

## 1. Purpose

This document lists the 2026 base salary bands for U.S. Tier-1 locations (San Jose). Austin bands are 90% of Tier-1, and Toronto bands are set separately in CAD. This information is confidential and must not be shared outside People Operations and the department's leadership.

## 2. Engineering Salary Bands (USD, annual base)

| Level | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Software Engineer I | $118,000 | $135,000 | $152,000 |
| E2 | Software Engineer II | $140,000 | $162,000 | $184,000 |
| E3 | Senior Software Engineer | $172,000 | $198,000 | $224,000 |
| E4 | Staff Software Engineer | $210,000 | $241,000 | $272,000 |

## 3. Merit Increase Budget

The 2026 merit increase budget is 3.5% of base payroll. The promotion budget is an additional 1.0%. Employees rated 4 or 5 are targeted for increases between 5% and 9%; employees rated 3 between 2% and 4%; employees rated 1 or 2 receive no merit increase.

## 4. Equity Refresh

Annual equity refresh grants are awarded to employees rated 4 or 5 and are approved by the Compensation Committee in November.
