# Information Security Acceptable Use Policy

Document ID: GEN-002 | Department: GENERAL | Category: POLICY | Owner: Information Security | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-02-01

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines acceptable use of Helios Systems information systems, including laptops, email, cloud services, source code repositories, and customer data. It applies to all employees, contractors, and interns.

## 2. Passwords and Authentication

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems.
- All employees must use the company password manager (1Password) to store work credentials.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, GitHub, AWS, and the HR portal. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with production AWS access.
- Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule, but must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.
- Never share passwords, MFA codes, or recovery codes with anyone, including IT staff.

## 3. Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled, automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity, and the endpoint protection agent running at all times. Personal devices may access email and chat only through the managed mobile app. Personal devices may never store customer data.

## 4. Data Classification

| Class | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Marketing website, press releases | No restrictions |
| Internal | Policies, SOPs, org charts | Share only with employees |
| Confidential | Customer data, financials, contracts, salaries | Need-to-know access, encrypted at rest and in transit |
| Restricted | Credentials, encryption keys, litigation material | Named individuals only, never in email or chat |

Confidential and Restricted data must never be pasted into public AI tools or consumer chatbots. Only company-approved AI tools that keep data inside Helios systems may be used with Internal or Confidential data.

## 5. Reporting Security Incidents

Suspected phishing emails must be reported using the "Report Phish" button in the email client or by forwarding them to security@helios.example.com. Lost devices, suspected malware, or accidental data exposure must be reported to the Security team within 1 hour of discovery. Employees will never be penalized for reporting a mistake in good faith.

## 6. Monitoring and Enforcement

Helios may monitor company systems for security purposes. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
