# New Employee Onboarding SOP

Document ID: HR-002 | Department: HR | Category: SOP | Owner: People Operations | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-03-01

## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes the steps People Operations, IT, and the hiring manager take to onboard a new employee. The goal is that every new hire is productive by the end of their second week.

## 2. Before Day One (Pre-boarding)

1. People Operations sends the welcome email and Workday account 7 calendar days before the start date.
2. IT ships the laptop so it arrives at least 2 business days before the start date.
3. The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the same team and schedules a 30-minute welcome call for Day 1.
4. The new hire completes the I-9 form (U.S.) and tax forms in Workday before Day 1.

## 3. Day One Checklist

- 9:00 AM: Welcome session with People Operations (benefits overview, handbook).
- 10:30 AM: IT setup — password manager, MFA enrollment, VPN, email.
- 1:00 PM: Lunch with onboarding buddy (expensed up to $40).
- 2:30 PM: 1:1 with hiring manager to review the 30-60-90 day plan.
- Complete mandatory Security Awareness training before end of Day 1.

## 4. First 90 Days

| Milestone | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Day 5 | All accounts working, handbook acknowledged, team introductions done |
| Day 30 | Completed role-specific training; first small deliverable shipped |
| Day 60 | Owns a recurring responsibility; first 1:1 feedback session with manager |
| Day 90 | Formal 90-day check-in with manager and HR; probation ends |

Mandatory trainings must be completed within 30 days: Security Awareness, Harassment Prevention (2 hours, required by California law), Data Privacy Basics, and the Code of Conduct.

## 5. Benefits Enrollment

New employees must enroll in medical, dental, and vision benefits within 30 days of their start date. Coverage begins on the first day of employment. Missing the 30-day window means waiting until the next open enrollment period in November, unless a qualifying life event occurs.

## 6. Onboarding Buddy Responsibilities

The buddy meets the new hire at least twice a week during the first month, answers day-to-day questions, and introduces them to at least five people outside their team.
