# FY2026 Q3 Budget Summary (Confidential)

Document ID: FIN-002 | Department: FINANCE | Category: REPORT | Owner: FP&A | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-10-05 | Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — Finance only

## 1. Headline Numbers

Q3 FY2026 (July to September) revenue was $38.4 million, 6% above plan and 18% higher than Q3 FY2025. Operating expenses were $33.1 million, 2% under budget. Operating margin was 13.8%.

| Department | Budget | Actual | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering | $14.2M | $13.9M | -2.1% |
| Sales and Marketing | $10.5M | $10.8M | +2.9% |
| General and Administrative | $5.1M | $4.7M | -7.8% |
| Customer Success | $3.9M | $3.7M | -5.1% |

## 3. Q4 Outlook

The Q4 revenue forecast is $40.2 million. Hiring for Q4 is limited to 22 approved backfills and 9 new roles, all in Engineering and Customer Success. Cloud infrastructure costs are forecast at $2.6 million for Q4 after the reserved-instance renewal.
