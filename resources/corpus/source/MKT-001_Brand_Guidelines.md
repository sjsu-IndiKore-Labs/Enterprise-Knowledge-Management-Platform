# Brand Guidelines

Document ID: MKT-001 | Department: MARKETING | Category: GUIDE | Owner: Brand Team | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-02-01

## 1. Logo

Always use the official logo files from the Brand Assets folder. Keep a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "H" in the wordmark. The minimum logo width is 120 pixels on screen and 25 millimeters in print. Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo.

## 2. Colors

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Helios Orange | #F26B1D | Primary brand color, calls to action |
| Midnight Navy | #14213D | Headlines and dark backgrounds |
| Cloud Gray | #E5E5E5 | Backgrounds and dividers |
| Signal Teal | #1B998B | Charts and secondary accents |

## 3. Typography

The primary typeface is Inter for all digital and print materials. Headlines use Inter Bold; body text uses Inter Regular at a minimum of 14 pixels on screen. Use Georgia only for long-form printed reports.

## 4. Voice and Tone

Helios writes clearly, confidently, and without jargon. Prefer short sentences and active voice. Write "supply chain" as two words and "Helios" without "Inc." except in legal text. Never use competitor names in marketing materials without Legal approval.
