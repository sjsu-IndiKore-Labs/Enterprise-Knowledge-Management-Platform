# Remote Work Policy

Document ID: GEN-003 | Department: GENERAL | Category: POLICY | Owner: People Operations | Version: 2 | Effective: 2026-07-01

Change note: Version 2 increases remote days from 2 to 3 per week, reduces anchor days to Tuesday and Wednesday, raises the internet stipend to $60 per month, and extends work-from-anywhere to 4 weeks per year.

## 1. Hybrid Schedule

Employees assigned to an office (San Jose, Austin, or Toronto) work on a hybrid schedule. Hybrid employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesday and Wednesday are designated in-office anchor days, and employees are expected in the office on both anchor days each week unless they are traveling for business or on approved leave.

## 2. Fully Remote Roles

Fully remote roles must be approved by a Vice President and by People Operations. Fully remote employees must live in a U.S. state or Canadian province where Helios is registered as an employer. Fully remote employees are expected to attend the quarterly team on-site, with travel paid by Helios.

## 3. Home Office Requirements

Remote employees must have a private workspace and a reliable internet connection of at least 100 Mbps download. Helios reimburses up to $60 per month for internet service for employees who work remotely at least one day per week. The one-time home-office equipment allowance is described in the Employee Handbook.

## 4. Working From Another Location

Employees may work from a location other than their home for up to 4 weeks per calendar year with manager approval. Working from outside the United States or Canada is not permitted without written approval from Legal and People Operations because of tax and data-protection requirements.
