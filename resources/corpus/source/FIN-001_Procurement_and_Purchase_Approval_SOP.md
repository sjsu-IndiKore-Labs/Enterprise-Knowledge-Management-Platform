# Procurement and Purchase Approval SOP

Document ID: FIN-001 | Department: FINANCE | Category: SOP | Owner: Procurement | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-02-01

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines how Helios employees buy goods and services from external vendors and who must approve each purchase.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

| Total contract value | Required approvals |
|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | Manager |
| $1,000 to $24,999 | Manager and Director |
| $25,000 to $99,999 | Director, Vice President, and Finance Business Partner |
| $100,000 or more | Vice President and Chief Financial Officer (CFO) |

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid a threshold is prohibited.

## 3. Competitive Quotes

Purchases of $10,000 or more require at least three written quotes from different vendors, unless the vendor is a sole source and a Sole Source Justification form is approved by Procurement.

## 4. Purchase Orders

A purchase order (PO) must be issued in NetSuite before any work begins or any goods ship. Invoices received without a valid PO number are returned to the vendor. Standard payment terms are Net 45.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 5. New Vendor Onboarding

New vendors must complete the vendor onboarding form, provide a W-9 (U.S.) or W-8BEN (non-U.S.), and pass a security review by Information Security if they will process Confidential data. Vendor onboarding typically takes 10 business days.

## 6. Software Purchases

All software and SaaS subscriptions, regardless of cost, must be reviewed by IT and Information Security before purchase to prevent shadow IT. Free trials that require entering company data also require this review.
