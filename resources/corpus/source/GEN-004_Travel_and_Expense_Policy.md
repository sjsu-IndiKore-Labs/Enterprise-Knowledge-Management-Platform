# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: GEN-004 | Department: GENERAL | Category: POLICY | Owner: Finance (Controller) | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-01-15

## 1. General Principles

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable, necessary business expenses. Spend company money as if it were your own. All expenses must be submitted in the Expensify system with an itemized receipt for any single expense of $25 or more.

## 2. Submission Deadline

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Reports submitted more than 60 days after the expense date will not be reimbursed except with written approval from the Controller. Approved reimbursements are paid by direct deposit within 10 business days.

## 3. Travel Booking

All flights, hotels, and rental cars must be booked through the Navan travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires Vice President approval.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 4. Spending Limits

| Expense | Limit |
|---|---|
| Hotel (standard city) | $250 per night |
| Hotel (high-cost city: NYC, SF, Boston, London, Toronto) | $375 per night |
| Meals while traveling (per diem) | $75 per day |
| Client entertainment | $150 per person, Director approval |
| Rideshare / taxi | Actual cost, standard tier only |
| Personal car mileage | IRS rate ($0.70 per mile in 2026) |

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursed: alcohol when not entertaining a client, in-flight entertainment, traffic or parking fines, personal travel extensions, upgrades not approved in advance, spa or gym fees, and lost-luggage fees for checked bags beyond one per trip.

## 6. Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than four times per year may request a corporate card. Corporate card statements must be reconciled in Expensify by the 5th business day of each month. Personal purchases on a corporate card are prohibited and must be repaid within 5 business days if made by mistake.
