# Data Retention and Privacy Policy

Document ID: LEG-002 | Department: LEGAL | Category: POLICY | Owner: Privacy Office | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-03-15

## 1. Retention Schedule

| Record type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Financial and tax records | 7 years |
| Employee personnel files | 7 years after termination |
| Customer contracts | 6 years after expiration |
| Customer production data | Duration of contract + 90 days |
| Application and security logs | 1 year |
| Recruiting records for candidates not hired | 2 years |
| Email | 3 years, unless under legal hold |

## 2. Legal Holds

When Legal issues a legal hold, all relevant records must be preserved regardless of the retention schedule until Legal releases the hold in writing. Deleting records under legal hold is a serious violation.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 3. Data Subject Requests

Requests from individuals to access, correct, or delete their personal data (under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or PIPEDA) must be forwarded to privacy@helios.example.com within 2 business days of receipt. The Privacy Office must respond within 30 days for GDPR requests and within 45 days for CCPA/CPRA requests.

## 4. Data Breaches

Any suspected breach of personal data must be reported to the Security team immediately. The Privacy Office decides whether regulators must be notified; under GDPR the deadline is 72 hours after becoming aware of the breach.

## 5. Privacy by Design

New products and features that process personal data require a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) before launch. The PIA template is available in the Legal space of the knowledge portal.
