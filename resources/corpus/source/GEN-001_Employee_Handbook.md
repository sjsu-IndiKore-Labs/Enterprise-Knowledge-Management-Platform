# Helios Systems Employee Handbook

Document ID: GEN-001 | Department: GENERAL | Category: GUIDE | Owner: People Operations | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. About Helios Systems

Helios Systems Inc. is a B2B software company headquartered at 2100 Orchard Parkway, San Jose, California. Founded in 2011, Helios builds supply-chain analytics software used by more than 400 manufacturing customers. As of January 2026 the company employs approximately 850 people across offices in San Jose, Austin, and Toronto, plus fully remote employees in 14 U.S. states.

Our values are Customer Obsession, Ownership, Clarity, and Respect. Every employee is expected to read this handbook during their first week and to acknowledge it in the HR portal within 10 business days of their start date.

## 2. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM local time, Monday through Friday, with a 30-minute unpaid lunch break. Core collaboration hours, when all employees are expected to be reachable, are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific Time. Non-exempt (hourly) employees must record all hours worked in the timekeeping system by 11:59 PM every Friday. Overtime for non-exempt employees must be pre-approved in writing by their manager.

## 3. Company Holidays

Helios observes 11 paid company holidays per year: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. In addition, each employee receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year that can be used for any personal, cultural, or religious observance. Floating holidays do not carry over to the next year.

## 4. Code of Conduct

All employees must act with honesty and integrity. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind is prohibited. Gifts from vendors or customers valued above $100 must be declined or reported to the Legal team using the Gift Disclosure form. Conflicts of interest, including outside employment with a competitor, must be disclosed to your manager and HR before the activity begins.

Concerns can be raised with your manager, with HR at hr-help@helios.example.com, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0142, which is available 24 hours a day and operated by an independent third party.

## 5. Equipment and Workspace

Every employee receives a company laptop on day one. Engineering staff receive a 16-inch MacBook Pro; all other staff receive a 14-inch MacBook Air or a Dell Latitude, based on preference. Employees may expense up to $500 once every two years for home-office equipment such as monitors, chairs, or desks. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours.

## 6. Dress Code

Helios has a business-casual dress code in the office. On customer-facing days, employees meeting customers in person should dress to the customer's standard.
