# Remote Work Policy

Document ID: GEN-003 | Department: GENERAL | Category: POLICY | Owner: People Operations | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-03-01

## 1. Hybrid Schedule

Employees assigned to an office (San Jose, Austin, or Toronto) work on a hybrid schedule. Hybrid employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are designated in-office anchor days, and employees must be in the office on at least three of these days each week.

## 2. Fully Remote Roles

Fully remote roles must be approved by a Vice President and by People Operations. Fully remote employees must live in a U.S. state or Canadian province where Helios is registered as an employer.

## 3. Home Office Requirements

Remote employees must have a private workspace and a reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps download. Helios reimburses up to $40 per month for internet service for employees who work remotely at least one day per week.

## 4. Working From Another Location

Employees may work from a location other than their home for up to 2 weeks per calendar year with manager approval. Working from outside the United States or Canada is not permitted without written approval from Legal and People Operations because of tax and data-protection requirements.
